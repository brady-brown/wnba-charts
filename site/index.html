How it's built
194,190 stints Every lineup change across 6,916 games from 1997 to today, reconstructed from play-by-play. Box score included Even the counting stats are derived from the play-by-play, so rebound rates and usage divide by what really happened on the floor rather than a share of minutes. One ridge penalty Tuned once by cross-validation and held fixed, so seasons compare directly.
